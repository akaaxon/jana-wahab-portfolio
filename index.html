05 — In practice

Work-Based Learning Reflection

Bridging Academic Theory and Real-World Operations

My academic journey as a Management Information Systems student has consistently emphasized how technology and business operations intersect to drive organizational efficiency. However, translating these theoretical concepts into practical application required stepping into a dynamic professional environment. My internship experience as a Junior Accountant and Administrative Intern at PuffZone provided the ideal platform to bridge this gap, allowing me to observe firsthand how information systems, financial management, and administrative workflows operate in a fast-paced business setting.

Working at PuffZone exposed me to a diverse range of day-to-day responsibilities that spanned accounting, human resources, and general administration. This multifaceted role demanded adaptability and a solid grasp of both technical tools and foundational business principles. Rather than operating in isolated silos, my daily tasks required me to synthesize my coursework in MIS, accounting, and financial management to support the company's broader operational goals.

Administrative and Accounting Responsibilities at PuffZone

A significant portion of my day-to-day work at PuffZone centered on data organization, administrative oversight, and financial record-keeping. Utilizing Microsoft Excel, I managed attendance tracking, scheduling, and working hours for a team of over 20 employees. This responsibility required a high degree of accuracy and attention to detail. Managing personnel data reinforced the critical link between data integrity and business operations; I quickly learned that a minor oversight in recording employee hours could directly impact payroll processing, resulting in administrative delays and employee dissatisfaction.

In addition to attendance and scheduling, my role as a Junior Accountant gave me hands-on exposure to core financial workflows. I assisted with cash management reports, organized financial records, and contributed to routine accounting tasks. Reconciling numbers and tracking financial inputs highlighted why accurate information systems are indispensable. Maintaining organized financial and administrative records ensures that management can make informed decisions quickly, minimizing operational bottlenecks and financial discrepancies.

Developing Professional Competencies

Beyond technical and administrative duties, my time at PuffZone—supplemented by my coursework, including specialized training through the WRN course—significantly contributed to my professional development.

  • Leadership & Communication: Through my coursework and hands-on administrative duties at PuffZone, I cultivated strong leadership and professional communication skills. Coordinating schedules and communicating policy updates to team members required clarity, empathy, and assertiveness.
  • Problem-Solving & Adaptability: Working in a live business environment often presented unexpected challenges, from resolving scheduling conflicts to addressing discrepancies in administrative reports. I learned to approach these issues methodically—analyzing the root cause before implementing practical, efficient solutions.
  • Time Management: Balancing a full university course load with active internship responsibilities tested and refined my organizational skills. Prioritizing competing deadlines ensured that all administrative and accounting deliverables were completed accurately and on time.

Career Aspirations and Future Outlook

Reflecting on my experience at PuffZone, my career interests have become much clearer. I have discovered a strong preference for roles that sit at the intersection of business operations, data analytics, and financial management. While my MIS degree equips me with the technical foundation needed to understand and evaluate business systems, my practical accounting and administrative experience has given me a grounded perspective on the financial realities of running an organization.

Looking ahead, I aim to pursue professional opportunities where I can leverage both my technical skills and my business background. Whether working in business analysis, information systems management, or corporate accounting, I am eager to continue building a career that bridges technology and business strategy to drive organizational success.
